About Nigeria Opportunity Alert

Nigeria Opportunity Alert is an independent information platform created to help people discover useful opportunities in Nigeria and internationally.

We organize publicly available opportunities into categories such as jobs, scholarships, internships, fellowships, training and volunteering.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to make opportunity information easier to discover while encouraging visitors to verify important details directly with the organization offering the opportunity.

Official Sources

Whenever possible, our listings direct users to the official website or application platform of the organization providing the opportunity.

Important Notice

Nigeria Opportunity Alert is not a government agency, recruitment agency, NGO, university, scholarship provider or representative of the organizations whose opportunities may appear on this website.

Users should independently confirm eligibility, deadlines, requirements and application procedures before applying.
