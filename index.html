⚠️ Stay Safe From Opportunity Scams

Nigeria Opportunity Alert is an information platform, not a government agency, NGO, recruitment company or scholarship provider. We do not guarantee selection. Never send money or sensitive information simply because someone promises a job, grant or scholarship. Verify opportunities through their official source before applying.
