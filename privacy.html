Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Nigeria Opportunity Alert respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website and how that information may be used.

1. About This Website

Nigeria Opportunity Alert is an independent information platform that publishes information about jobs, scholarships, fellowships, internships, volunteering, grants and training opportunities.

We are not a government agency, recruitment agency, scholarship provider, NGO or representative of the organizations whose opportunities may appear on this website.

2. Information We Collect

We do not intentionally require visitors to create an account or submit sensitive personal information simply to browse opportunities.

Depending on how the website is used, limited technical information may be processed automatically by hosting, analytics or advertising services. This may include:

  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • General location information
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of visits
  • Basic technical information

3. Cookies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve functionality, understand website usage and, where applicable, support advertising services.

Cookies are small files stored on your device by websites. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, although some website functions may be affected.

4. Advertising

Nigeria Opportunity Alert may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, if the website becomes eligible and is approved for such services.

Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display advertisements based on factors such as a user's visit to this and other websites.

Users should review the privacy policies of relevant advertising providers for more information about how advertising data is handled.

5. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to official websites and other third-party websites.

When you click an external link, you leave Nigeria Opportunity Alert. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security or policies of third-party websites.

We recommend reading the privacy policy of any external website before providing personal information.

6. Opportunity Applications

Nigeria Opportunity Alert does not process applications for the organizations listed on our website unless explicitly stated otherwise.

When you click an official application link, information you provide is submitted directly to the relevant organization or platform according to its own policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Visitors should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information online.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of the website. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Your Choices

You may control cookies through your browser settings. You may also choose not to follow external links or submit information to third-party websites.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the website, you may contact the website administrator through the contact method provided on the website.

Important:

Always verify job, scholarship, grant, fellowship and other opportunity information through the official organization before applying or providing personal information.
