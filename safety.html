Safety & Scam Prevention

Nigeria Opportunity Alert is an independent information platform that helps visitors discover publicly available opportunities.

⚠️ Important Safety Notice

Always verify an opportunity through the official website of the organization offering it before submitting personal information or making any payment.

Common Warning Signs

Be careful if an opportunity or person:

  • Asks you to pay money through an unofficial account, personal bank account or unknown payment channel.
  • Requests your bank PIN, OTP, password or other confidential security information.
  • Uses a suspicious website or a domain unrelated to the organization supposedly offering the opportunity.
  • Promises guaranteed employment, admission, funding or selection in exchange for money.
  • Pressures you to send money or personal information immediately.
  • Contacts you through an unofficial social-media account while claiming to represent a major organization.

Before You Apply

  • Open the official source provided on the opportunity page.
  • Confirm the organization's name and website address.
  • Check the eligibility requirements carefully.
  • Check the official application deadline.
  • Make sure the application is being submitted through the organization's official platform.
  • Never share your password, PIN or OTP with a recruiter or unknown person.
🔎 Verify Before You Trust

Nigeria Opportunity Alert aims to direct visitors toward official sources. However, external organizations can change their websites, deadlines, requirements or application procedures.

Always rely on the current information published by the official organization before taking action.

We Do Not Guarantee Selection

Listing an opportunity on Nigeria Opportunity Alert does not mean that we are connected with the organization offering it. We do not guarantee employment, admission, funding, scholarship awards, recruitment or selection.

Report an Incorrect Opportunity

If you find an outdated opportunity, incorrect link or other problem with a listing, please contact us through our Contact page.

External Websites

When you click an official opportunity button, you may leave Nigeria Opportunity Alert and visit an external website. Review that website's own terms, privacy policy and security information before providing personal information.
